About Tanglin Dental

Building healthier smiles since 1994

Tanglin Dental Surgeons has served patients in Singapore since 1994. Its practice brings together general dentistry and specialist services, giving patients access to a broad range of dental care.

With seven clinic locations, the practice aims to make dental appointments more accessible for individuals and families across the island.
