# Tanglin Dental Surgeons — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Tanglin Dental Surgeons is a Singapore-based dental practice established in 1994, operating seven clinics across Orchard, Bishan, Yishun, Boon Lay, Clementi, Tampines, and Bedok. The practice provides family and specialist dentistry, including orthodontics, Invisalign, restorative treatments, oral surgery, children's dentistry, and preventive dental care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a modern, approachable, and professionally credible digital experience that highlights the practice's established history, specialist expertise, comprehensive services, and islandwide clinic accessibility.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Patient Journey and Treatment Discovery

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage presented a broad range of dental services but needed a more direct pathway connecting visitors' dental concerns with relevant treatments and appointment actions.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised treatment information into clearer, patient-focused pathways covering routine care, orthodontics, restorative dentistry, oral surgery, children's dentistry, and aesthetic treatments. Prominent treatment links and appointment actions help visitors identify suitable services and navigate towards the next step more efficiently.

### 2. Improved Seven-Clinic Discovery and Navigation

**Previous Issue:** With seven clinic locations across Singapore, visitors needed a simpler way to identify nearby branches, access contact details, and navigate clinic information, particularly on mobile devices.

**Redesign Improvement:** Prioritised the seven clinic locations through a more organised presentation of branch names, addresses, and contact options. Clearer clinic navigation and mobile-friendly access make it easier for patients to select a convenient location and proceed with enquiries or appointment booking.

### 3. Stronger Trust and Specialist Expertise Communication

**Previous Issue:** The practice's long-standing history, specialist clinicians, and extensive treatment capabilities required greater prominence and a more cohesive presentation within the homepage.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the visibility of the practice's history since 1994, specialist dental expertise, clinical team information, and service range. The redesigned content hierarchy connects professional credibility with treatment information and appointment opportunities, helping visitors make more informed decisions.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a more structured progression from the homepage introduction to treatments, specialist information, clinic locations, and booking actions.
- **Improved Treatment Navigation:** Organised dental services into accessible, patient-focused categories for easier browsing.
- **Stronger Brand Presentation:** Maintained the practice's blue-led visual identity and orange accents within a cleaner, more contemporary design direction.
- **Enhanced Professional Credibility:** Increased the prominence of specialist expertise, clinical team information, and the practice's established history.
- **Simplified Clinic Discovery:** Improved the organisation and accessibility of information across all seven locations.
- **Responsive User Experience:** Prioritised readable layouts, clear navigation, and convenient contact and appointment pathways on smaller screens.
- **Greater Visual Consistency:** Improved typography, spacing, content alignment, and overall presentation to establish a more cohesive homepage experience.

## Summary

The redesigned Tanglin Dental Surgeons homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: unclear treatment-to-booking pathways, complex seven-clinic navigation, and insufficient prominence of professional trust signals.

The improvements create a clearer, more informative, and patient-focused experience while reinforcing the practice's established reputation, specialist expertise, and accessibility across Singapore.
